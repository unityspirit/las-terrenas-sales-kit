# Las Terrenas & Samaná Business Intelligence (2024–2026)
**Prepared for Unity Spirit Partners Inc.**

## 1. Automation & Digital Services Missing in Las Terrenas
* **WhatsApp Business API & Chatbots (Huge Gap):** 95%+ of businesses use personal WhatsApp or the free manual app. They lack automated instant replies, AI FAQ answering (menus, directions, policies), and automated booking confirmations.
* **Integrated Direct Online Payment Gateways:** Businesses either rely entirely on Booking.com/Airbnb or ask guests for wire transfers/cash on arrival. They are missing automated card pre-authorizations and Azul/CardNET payment links on their own domains, costing them impulse bookings.
* **Email Marketing & Guest Lifecycle:** Almost completely absent among independent venues. No pre-arrival welcome emails (upselling shuttles/chef services), no post-checkout review generation, and no seasonal reactivation campaigns.
* **Table & Activity Online Reservation Systems:** Restaurants in Pueblo de los Pescadores lose reservations during dinner rush because they can't answer WhatsApp. Tour operators have no instant slot booking.

## 3. Top Business Pain Points (Your Sales Angles)
1. **Crushing OTA Commission Dependency:** Heavy dependence on Booking.com/Airbnb costs owners 15–25% of gross revenue. They desperately need direct booking funnels.
2. **Astronomical Electricity Costs:** Las Terrenas uses private power (Luz y Fuerza) which is incredibly expensive. Businesses need to increase margins (via direct bookings) to offset utility costs.
3. **Severe Seasonality:** Peak season (Dec–April) hits 90–95% occupancy, but low season drops to 35–45%. Businesses need digital marketing to survive the slow months.
4. **Labor & Language:** High turnover and a shortage of bilingual staff means businesses need automated multilingual websites to handle European and North American inquiries.
