Our Story

A sanctuary
unlike any other

Nestled along the pristine shores of Playa Bonita in Samaná, Villa Caribé Boutique Hotel is where barefoot luxury meets refined sophistication. Born from a love affair between French art de vivre and the vibrant spirit of the Dominican Republic.

Twelve exquisitely appointed suites, a cliff-top pool with panoramic ocean views, and a culinary experience that fuses Creole tradition with Parisian technique. This is not just a hotel — it is a feeling you will carry long after your return home.
