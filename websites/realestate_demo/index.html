Dominican Republic Law 158-01

The CONFOTUR
Tax Advantage

CONFOTUR is the Dominican Republic's flagship tourism investment incentive — designed to attract international capital by eliminating the most significant real estate taxes.

🏛️
0% Transfer Tax
No property transfer tax on the purchase of eligible CONFOTUR-certified real estate.
📋
Property Tax Exemption
Up to 15 years of annual property tax (IPI) exemption on approved developments.
💵
Capital Repatriation
Freely repatriate your capital and profits to your home country, tax-free.
🌍
Open to Foreigners
Non-Dominican nationals enjoy the exact same rights and tax benefits as locals.

Law 158-01 was enacted to incentivize tourism development across the Dominican Republic. Properties certified under CONFOTUR enjoy a comprehensive package of fiscal benefits, making DR one of the most investor-friendly jurisdictions in the Caribbean.

Why Las Terrenas?

Five compelling reasons why global investors are choosing the Samaná Peninsula as their Caribbean real estate destination.

01 🏆
Year of Samaná 2026

The Dominican government has designated 2026 the "Year of Samaná", channeling unprecedented infrastructure investment, tourism promotion, and international attention to the peninsula.

02 🏗️
Infrastructure Boom

New international airport expansions, road upgrades, and port facilities are transforming Samaná into a world-class tourism corridor, driving real estate demand.

03 🌐
International Community

Las Terrenas hosts one of the Caribbean's largest expat communities — French, Italian, Swiss, Canadian — with a vibrant cosmopolitan lifestyle year-round.

04 📈
Year-Round Rental Income

Unlike other Caribbean destinations, Samaná's micro-climate ensures high tourist occupancy throughout the year, delivering consistent rental yields of 6–9%.

05 💹
8–10% Appreciation

Las Terrenas has consistently delivered 8–10% annual property value growth over the last decade, outperforming most Caribbean markets and many European cities.
