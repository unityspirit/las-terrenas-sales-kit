Eco-Tourism Commitment

We Protect What We Share

Las Terrenas and the Samaná Peninsula are among the most biodiverse and pristine environments in the Caribbean. We believe adventure and conservation are inseparable — every experience we offer is designed to give back more than it takes.

🌱 Eco-Certified
🐋 Whale Watching Code
♻️ Plastic-Free Camp

🌊

Ocean Stewardship

Monthly reef cleanups with local dive schools. Biodegradable sunscreen required.

🌿

Trail Preservation

El Limón guided groups capped at 8 per trip to protect native flora and fauna.

🤝

Community First

80% of staff hired locally. We source meals from family farms in Las Terrenas.

🐋

Whale Respect Protocol

IWC-compliant whale watching. Engines off at 100m. No flash photography.
