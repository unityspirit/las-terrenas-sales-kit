Our Story

Two Decades of
Beachfront Magic

Born from a passionate encounter between French culinary tradition and the vibrant soul of the Dominican Caribbean, La Mer has graced the shores of Pueblo de los Pescadores for over twenty years — a haven where the rhythm of the waves dictates the pace of every meal.

Founded in 2004 by Chef Isabelle Fontaine and her Dominican partner Ramón Castillo, La Mer began as a modest beachside table and grew into one of Samaná's most celebrated dining destinations. Our philosophy is simple: honour the sea, respect the land, and nourish the soul.

Every dish is a dialogue — between Paris and the Caribbean, between centuries-old French technique and the bold, sun-kissed ingredients of the Dominican Republic. Our fish is caught each morning by local fishermen at Playa Las Ballenas. Our produce arrives daily from mountain farms in Los Ranchos.

— Isabelle & Ramón
